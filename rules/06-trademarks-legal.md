# Trademarks and legal rules

These rules apply to all ROCm documentation regarding trademark usage
and combined product naming.

## Trademark symbols

### Rule: ThirdPartyProductNaming
**Severity:** error
**Scope:** All documentation — combined product names
**Rule:** When combining AMD trademarks with third-party trademarks or product names, use the format "X for ROCm" or "X for AMD". Do not prefix the third-party name with AMD/ROCm.
**Wrong:**
```md
ROCm PyTorch
AMD TensorFlow
```
**Right:**
```md
PyTorch for ROCm
TensorFlow for ROCm
```
**Source:** ROCm custom
